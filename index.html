How a game works

When you create or join, you lock in a fingerprint of a secret that only you know. Once two players are in, both reveal their secrets. The two secrets together decide every roll, so neither player nor anyone running the network can predict or steer the dice.

Players roll in turn, starting with the player who created the game, until someone rolls a double. The creator rolls first and has a small edge, about 55% to win. If a player doesn't reveal within 24 hours, the other player can settle the game and take the pot. Winnings and refunds sit in the contract until you withdraw them.

This is an experiment. The contract hasn't been audited, so only play with small amounts you can afford to lose, and only with people you know.
